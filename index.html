FAQ

Questions we hear every day

How do I know the property documents are genuine?

Our team checks ownership papers, NOC and tax records before listing. You also get copies, so your own lawyer can review them.

Do you charge any hidden fees?

No. Our commission is explained at the start and written in the agreement. There are no surprise charges.

Can I visit a property before deciding?

Yes. Message us and we will arrange a viewing at a time that suits you, even on weekends.

Can overseas Pakistanis buy through you?

Yes. We offer video tours, document sharing on WhatsApp and a trusted representative to handle the process on your behalf.

Do you help with selling or renting my property?

Yes. We value your property at the current market rate, list it, find verified buyers or tenants and manage the agreement.

Is installment payment available?

On selected projects, yes. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will share the current plans.
